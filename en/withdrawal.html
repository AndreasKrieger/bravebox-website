Withdrawal information

Status: beta phase. This page will be extended with the statutory model form once paid licenses are introduced.

Beta: no purchase contract

BRAVE Box is provided free of charge during the beta. There is no paid purchase contract and therefore no refund claim. You can stop using it anytime by uninstalling (see also § 4 of the terms).

Paid offers (future)

Once paid licenses are offered, a 14-day right of withdrawal from conclusion of contract applies (§§ 355 et seq. BGB), with a model withdrawal form. For digital content, the right expires only with your express consent to immediate performance and your acknowledgement of expiry (§ 356(5) BGB).
