2. Overview of Data Processing

Local (no transmission): Your music files, DJ libraries, and user-specific settings (such as ratings, energy values, and play counts) are processed exclusively on your device and are not transmitted to external servers.

Transmitted (with your consent only): Anonymous usage and error data (telemetry) used solely to improve the software, as well as voluntary feedback messages. Details in section 3.

3. Telemetry and Error Analysis (with your consent only)

If you enable the separate, voluntary opt-in for telemetry in the consent dialog, the app sends anonymous usage data to PostHog Inc. (PostHog Cloud EU, servers in the EU).

The following data is transmitted:

  • Application events (app start, used features, completed conversions, number of converted tracks)
  • Error and crash reports (error type and sanitized stack traces; local paths, usernames, and line numbers are removed before transmission)
  • Technical information: app version, operating system, language setting
  • A random session identifier (no inference about your identity)
  • A country code derived server-side from your IP address (coarse geographic assignment). Your IP address is not stored; it is used only for this assignment and then discarded.

Legal basis: Your consent (Art. 6 (1) (a) GDPR, § 25 (1) TDDDG). You can withdraw your consent at any time in the settings; after withdrawal, no further telemetry data is sent.

Recipient: PostHog Inc. (processing on EU servers). PostHog Privacy Policy.

Retention period: Event data is stored for as long as it is required for the evaluation and is then deleted automatically.

4. Feedback (voluntary)

You can send us messages via the feedback form and optionally provide an email address for replies. Your message is sanitized (local paths and usernames are removed) and transmitted via PostHog.

Legal basis: Your consent (Art. 6 (1) (a) GDPR).

5. Software Updates (GitHub Releases)

The app automatically checks for updates and downloads them via GitHub Releases. Your IP address, client identifier (user agent), and the current app version are transmitted to GitHub Inc. (USA).

Legal basis: Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR (legitimate interest in secure and up-to-date software).
Recipient: GitHub Inc., USA (transfer based on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework).

6. Local Storage

The app stores configuration and metadata locally under %LOCALAPPDATA%\BRAVEBox:

  • .license (local beta license)
  • .instance (device ID)
  • .consent (your consent including the telemetry decision)
  • track_metadata_cache.json (your DJ metadata such as ratings, energy values, play counts)
  • startup_debug.log (technical logs; local only, not transmitted)

Legal basis: Art. 6 (1) (b) GDPR, § 25 (2) (2) TDDDG.

7. Your Rights (Art. 15–22 GDPR)

  • Access to the processed data
  • Rectification of inaccurate data
  • Erasure ("right to be forgotten")
  • Restriction of processing
  • Data portability
  • Objection to processing
  • Withdrawal of your consent (Art. 7 (3) GDPR)
  • Lodging a complaint with a supervisory authority

Contact: andreaskrieger.software@gmail.com

8. Data Deletion

You can delete local data by removing the %LOCALAPPDATA%\BRAVEBox folder. Uninstalling removes the app, but data stored in the %LOCALAPPDATA%\BRAVEBox folder remains and should be deleted manually if needed. Telemetry data stored with PostHog can be requested for deletion at any time via the contact address.
