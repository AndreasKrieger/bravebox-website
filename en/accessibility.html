Accessibility statement

Status: October 2026. The German Accessibility Strengthening Act (BFSG) applies since 28 June 2025.

Our standard

This website is a plain static page: semantic HTML, keyboard operability, visible focus rings, alternative texts for all images and respect for prefers-reduced-motion. The goal is alignment with WCAG 2.1 AA / EN 301 549.

Known limitations

The embedded app screenshots contain text as bitmaps; all essential content is additionally available as real text on this page. There are currently no videos requiring subtitles.

Feedback and contact

If you encounter barriers, please report them with the affected page and the assistive technology used to: andreaskrieger.software@gmail.com. We will address them as far as technically possible.

Conciliation

In disputes arising from the BFSG you may contact the conciliation body under § 16 BGG.
