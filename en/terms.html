2. Service

BRAVE Box is desktop software for analyzing and optimizing DJ libraries and playlists (Serato and Rekordbox). It provides features such as Camelot key matching, BPM linking, transition route creation, an interactive energy curve designer, and the export of modified playlists/crates. All data is processed locally on your device.
